## Market Share
- **Flutter**: ~46% market share among cross-platform mobile frameworks (2026).
- **React Native**: 35–38% market share.

## Adoption
- Both frameworks are widely used in production.
- **Flutter**: Adopted by Google (Google Ads), Alibaba, BMW, eBay Motors, Nubank, Grab, ByteDance, Philips Hue.
