- Both frameworks enable building cross-platform apps from a single codebase, reducing development time and costs by 30-60% compared to native development.
- **Flutter:** Supports mobile, web, desktop, and embedded platforms with first-party support.
- **React Native:** Primarily focused on mobile, with web and desktop support provided by the community.
