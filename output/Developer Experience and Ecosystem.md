### Developer Pool and Costs
- **React Native:** Larger developer pool and lower upfront costs due to JavaScript's popularity.
- **Flutter:** Developers are less common and command higher rates, but long-term maintenance costs are often lower.

### Package Ecosystem
- **Flutter:** pub.dev is smaller but more curated and stable.
- **React Native:** npm ecosystem is much larger but less curated, increasing flexibility and supply chain risk.

### Testing and Accessibility
- Both frameworks offer robust automated testing ecosystems:
  - Flutter: Built-in unit, widget, and integration testing.
  - React Native: Jest, Detox, and Maestro for unit, component, and end-to-end testing.
- Both provide strong accessibility support, integrating with platform-native assistive technologies.
