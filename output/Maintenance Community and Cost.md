- **Flutter:** Maintained by Google, regular updates, transparent roadmap, strong institutional backing, vibrant community.
- **React Native:** Maintained by Meta and community, strong ecosystem, APIs for host OS interaction.
- **Cost & Efficiency:** Both can cut dev time in half, reduce costs by up to 40%, maintain high performance and UX.
- **Migration:** Flutter migration can reduce maintenance time/cost, accelerate deployment, improve brand consistency.
